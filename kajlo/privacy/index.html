Privacy Policy for Kajlo

Last updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kajlo (“we,” “us,” or “the App”) handles information when you use the Kajlo iOS application (Bundle ID: com.lhtdev.test). Kajlo is an outdoor short-video community for discovering trail stories, publishing clips for review, joining official activities with Kajlo Credits, and chatting with offline scripted creator personas.

By using Kajlo, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

Contact: For privacy, safety, copyright, or account questions, email support@kajlo.app.

1. Summary

  • Most profile, content, chat, Credits balance, and tool data is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate a separate account server that receives your password or full profile for cloud sync in this version.
  • Camera, Microphone, Photos, Location, App Tracking Transparency (ATT), and Push Notifications are requested only as described below.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment card details.
  • Optional Agnes text-to-image generation sends only the prompt text you enter (and related generation parameters) to a third-party API. No chat LLM is used.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we process

2.1 Information you provide on device

When you use Quick Login and in-app features, Kajlo may store on your device:

  • Local sign-in credentials you create for this App (username/password stored for session unlock on this device only)
  • Profile fields: nickname, bio, optional avatar image file path
  • Trail videos you record or upload, cover images, publish drafts, and review status metadata
  • Favorites, “not interested” flags, blocked creators, reports you submit
  • Offline Compass chat threads and scripted message history
  • Kajlo Credits balance and a local spend/purchase ledger (amounts and timestamps; not card data)
  • Workout Log entries, Trail Goals, Gratitude Journal entries, and Inspiration Board cards (text and optional local images)
  • Preference flags such as whether ATT/Push prompts have already been shown

2.2 Information from Apple and the device

  • In-App Purchase receipts / StoreKit transaction results needed to credit Kajlo Credits after a successful purchase
  • Approximate location (when you allow Location) to support nearby trail-oriented browsing; public labels stay at area / route-segment precision — the App does not display exact home/work addresses or IP-based city tags on profiles
  • Advertising / tracking authorization status (ATT) when you respond to Apple’s system prompt
  • Push notification authorization status after you respond to Apple’s system prompt
  • Standard iOS diagnostics that remain on device unless you share them with Apple through system tools

2.3 Information sent to third-party AI (Agnes)

If you use Generate with Agnes on the Inspiration Board, the App sends:

  • The text prompt you typed describing an outdoor scene
  • Fixed generation parameters (model name, size/ratio settings)

The App does not send your password, full profile, chat history, Credits ledger, video library, or contact list to Agnes. Generated images returned by the API are saved as local files on your device for Inspiration / optional publish handoff.

See Section 5 for more detail.

3. How we use information

We use on-device and permission-related information to:

  • Operate Quick Login, session unlock, Log Out, and Delete Account
  • Show the My Trail feed, Outdoor hub, Publish / review flows, Messages (Compass), and Mine tools
  • Apply content safety filters, report, block, and hide blocked creators’ content
  • Deduct or add Kajlo Credits for eligible features and IAP packs
  • Request Camera, Microphone, Photos, Location, ATT, and Push only for the purposes stated in the system permission dialogs and this Policy
  • Generate optional Inspiration images via Agnes when you choose that feature

We do not use Kajlo Credits purchases to buy ranking, reach, review speed, or safety-checklist bypass.

4. Facial data

This section explains Kajlo’s practices regarding facial data for App Store Guideline 2.1 / 5.1.1 review.

4.1 What we collect

Kajlo does not create, store, or process biometric faceprints, face templates, or facial geometry for identification or authentication.

You may optionally capture or select photos or video frames that depict a human face (for example, a profile avatar from Camera or Photos, trail video frames, or Inspiration images that happen to include people). Those media files are ordinary images/video, not biometric identifiers.

4.2 Planned use

Any photo or video that may depict a face is used only to:

  • Display your chosen avatar or trail content inside the App
  • Support optional Inspiration Board cards and publish drafts you create
  • Allow you to review, delete, or replace that media

We do not use facial images for biometric login, emotion scoring, or identity matching against other users.

4.3 Sharing, disclosure, and where stored

Avatar and trail media that may depict a face are stored on your device (app sandbox / Documents as applicable). They are not uploaded by Kajlo to a Kajlo-operated cloud profile server in this version.

If you use Agnes image generation, only the text prompt is sent to Agnes; Kajlo does not upload your avatar or library face photos as Agnes reference images in the current implementation.

We do not sell facial imagery. Disclosure may occur only if required by law or if you yourself export/share media outside the App using the device share sheet.

4.4 Retention

Facial-depicting media remains on your device until you delete the related content, replace your avatar, clear the related Inspiration/video item, or use Delete Account, which clears Kajlo’s local app data domain for this Bundle ID.

4.5 Scope of this chapter

This Section 4 is the complete explanation of Kajlo’s collection, use, disclosure, sharing, and retention practices for facial data (including that we do not collect biometric faceprints).

7. In-App Purchases and Kajlo Credits

Kajlo offers consumable Kajlo Credits packs via Apple StoreKit (eight tiers, price-ascending). Example Product IDs include com.lhtdev.lv1 through com.kajlo.credits8. Payment is processed by Apple. We do not collect or store credit card numbers.

Credits may be spent on features such as submit/review gates, official activity joins, and Reel Template use as shown in the App. Credits never purchase exposure, ranking, review speed, or safety bypass.

There is no Restore Purchases control; consumable Credits are granted on successful transaction completion on the device.

8. Account controls

  • Quick Login — creates/uses a local session on this device
  • Log Out — ends the session; local data may remain until deleted
  • Delete Account — clears Kajlo’s persistent domain for Bundle ID com.lhtdev.test, including profile, videos metadata, chats, Credits display/ledger UI data, tools content, and related local preferences, then returns you to a fresh state

Apple’s payment records remain under Apple’s systems according to Apple’s policies.

9. Children’s privacy

Kajlo is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data in the App, contact support@kajlo.app so we can help you remove local data via Delete Account guidance.

10. Data security

We rely on iOS app sandbox storage and standard device protections. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Keep your device locked and updated.

11. International users

Kajlo is intended for distribution via the US App Store. If you use the App from another region, you do so on your own initiative and are responsible for compliance with local laws.

12. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
